DEFENDER PLAYBOOK

Interrupt the attack chain

Three controls that work across synthetic phishing, voice cloning, and automated discovery.

01

Pause the pressure

Urgency, secrecy, and unusual requests are signals to stop the workflow — not reasons to speed up.

02

Verify independently

Use a trusted directory, known number, or established process outside the suspicious message.

03

Report the pattern

Preserve the fictional evidence and share it with defenders so the next attempt is easier to catch.
